Frequently asked questions

Does it cost anything?

No. %BRAND% is free and supported by unobtrusive ads.

Does everyone need an account?

No. Whoever creates the trip adds everyone's names. People who join with the code pick their name; the rest stay in the group and someone logs their expenses. To mark or confirm their own payments, each person joins with the code.

Which currency are debts paid in?

When creating the trip you choose the currency you'll pay each other in. When splitting, debts are converted at the day's rate, which you can edit.

What is a partial split?

Settling up at any point during the trip. Those expenses are closed and don't count again in the final split, but they still show in the overall summary.

Does %BRAND% move money?

No. It only calculates and keeps records. You pay through your own bank or wallet and confirm it in the app.

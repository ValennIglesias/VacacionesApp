Before you write

  • Can't sign in: tap "Forgot your password?" on the sign-in screen and we'll email you a link to choose a new one.
  • Questions about how it works: see the FAQ and the guides.
  • Deleting your account: you can do it yourself in the app, under "My trips", at the bottom of the list.

What you can write to us about

  • Support: something isn't working as expected. Tell us what happened and, if you can, on which device.
  • Privacy: request a copy of your data or ask how we handle it. See the privacy policy.
  • Security: if you found a vulnerability, please email us before disclosing it and we'll review it as a priority.
  • Press and partnerships: articles, collaborations or integrations.
